nivel: "Nivel 1 · Filtros y funciones"
title: "Empleados contratados en 2005, con la fecha en formato DD/MM/YYYY."
---

```sql
SELECT first_name || ' ' || last_name AS empleado,
       TO_CHAR(hire_date, 'DD/MM/YYYY') AS fecha_ingreso
FROM   employees
WHERE  EXTRACT(YEAR FROM hire_date) = 2005
ORDER BY hire_date;
```
