---
nivel: "Nivel 2 · Agrupación y JOINs"
title: "Empleados contratados antes que su jefe."
---

```sql
SELECT e.last_name AS empleado, e.hire_date AS ingreso_empleado,
       m.last_name AS jefe,     m.hire_date AS ingreso_jefe
FROM   employees e
JOIN   employees m ON e.manager_id = m.employee_id
WHERE  e.hire_date < m.hire_date
ORDER BY e.hire_date;
```
