---
nivel: "Nivel 3 · Subconsultas y conjuntos"
title: "El departamento con el salario promedio más alto."
---

```sql
SELECT d.department_name, ROUND(AVG(e.salary)) AS promedio
FROM   employees e
JOIN   departments d ON e.department_id = d.department_id
GROUP BY d.department_name
HAVING AVG(e.salary) = (SELECT MAX(AVG(salary))
                        FROM   employees
                        GROUP BY department_id);
```
