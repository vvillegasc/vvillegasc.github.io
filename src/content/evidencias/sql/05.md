---
nivel: "Nivel 2 · Agrupación y JOINs"
title: "Por cargo: empleados, salario promedio, mínimo y máximo (solo cargos con más de 3 personas)."
---

```sql
SELECT j.job_title AS cargo,
       COUNT(*) AS empleados,
       ROUND(AVG(e.salary)) AS promedio,
       MIN(e.salary) AS minimo,
       MAX(e.salary) AS maximo
FROM   employees e
JOIN   jobs j ON e.job_id = j.job_id
GROUP BY j.job_title
HAVING COUNT(*) > 3
ORDER BY promedio DESC;
```
