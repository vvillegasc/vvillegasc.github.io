title: "Ingreso anual con comisión y años de antigüedad."
---

```sql
SELECT last_name,
       salary,
       NVL(commission_pct, 0) AS comision,
       salary * 12 * (1 + NVL(commission_pct, 0)) AS ingreso_anual,
       TRUNC(MONTHS_BETWEEN(SYSDATE, hire_date) / 12) AS anios_servicio
FROM   employees
ORDER BY anios_servicio DESC;
```
