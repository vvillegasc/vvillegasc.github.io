nivel: "Nivel 1 · Filtros y funciones"
title: "Correo corporativo de los empleados con apellido de más de 6 letras."
---

```sql
SELECT INITCAP(first_name) || ' ' || UPPER(last_name) AS empleado,
       LOWER(email) || '@empresa.com' AS correo,
       LENGTH(last_name) AS letras_apellido
FROM   employees
WHERE  LENGTH(last_name) > 6
ORDER BY letras_apellido DESC, last_name;
```
