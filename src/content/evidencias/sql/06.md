---
nivel: "Nivel 2 · Agrupación y JOINs"
title: "Porcentaje del salario máximo de su cargo que gana cada empleado."
---

```sql
SELECT e.last_name, j.job_title, e.salary, j.max_salary,
       ROUND(e.salary / j.max_salary * 100, 1) AS pct_del_maximo
FROM   employees e
JOIN   jobs j ON e.job_id = j.job_id
ORDER BY pct_del_maximo DESC;
```
