---
nivel: "Nivel 2 · Agrupación y JOINs"
title: "Por ciudad: departamentos y empleados, contando los departamentos vacíos."
---

```sql
SELECT l.city,
       COUNT(DISTINCT d.department_id) AS departamentos,
       COUNT(e.employee_id) AS empleados
FROM   locations l
JOIN   departments d ON d.location_id = l.location_id
LEFT JOIN employees e ON e.department_id = d.department_id
GROUP BY l.city
ORDER BY empleados DESC;
```
